[h1]RimSynapse - Aura Storyteller Algorithm[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Core/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]The narrative director that dynamically shifts threats based on your choices and sanity using local Large Language Models.[/b]

Meet Aura (The AI Storyteller!)
Aura Algorithm actively monitors the narrative threads of your colony. She does not just throw random events at you; she watches your choices, evaluates the psychological state of your pawns, and crafts bespoke scenarios and quests to test your morals and sanity. Her threat scaling uses traditional colony wealth as a baseline logical cost-benefit analysis, but the frequency and type of threats are dynamically orchestrated.

[i]NOTE: Aura requires an active LLM connection to orchestrate her narrative intelligence. If the LLM becomes disconnected or unresponsive, her storytelling logic will automatically fall back to Classic Cassandra.[/i]

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Morality-Based Storytelling:[/b] Quests and threat types scale based on psychological tensions rather than just raw silver wealth.
[*] [b]Cassandra Fallback Protection:[/b] Storytelling logic automatically shifts back to Classic Cassandra if the LLM server goes offline.
[/list]
